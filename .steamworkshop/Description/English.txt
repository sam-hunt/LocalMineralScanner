Local Mineral Scanner

Never strip-mine again.

[h1]About[/h1]
A compact, pawn-operated 2x2 scanner that reveals undiscovered mineral deposits on the map you are already on. Same work per find as the ground-penetrating scanner (tunable default), in a smaller footprint with room for two operators.

Perfect for that clutch moment you run out of components, enemy settlement raids, or asteroid mining opportunities when little-no ore spawns exposed.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Each find unfogs a contiguous ore vein that existing generation/events placed; nothing new is spawned
[*]Tune it to any mineable ore type, vanilla or modded
[*]Two pawns can operate it at once, each at full research speed
[*]Minifiable to fit in a caravan, shuttle, or transport-pod if needed
[*]No new research: unlocked by vanilla's long-range mineral scanner project
[*]Mod settings for the roof rule, scan times, uninstallation, build cost, and mass
[/list]

[b]Q: Isn't this imbalanced?[/b]
A: That's up to you. With gravship-travel in mind, it's much more space-efficient than Vanilla's long-distance scanner, but default find work cost is equivalent to the ground-penetrating scanner. If you want it harder, stretch the scan times or raise the costs via mod settings. If you want it easier, wind them down. Your colony, your rules.

[b]Q: Can it find ore added by other mods?[/b]
A: Yes. Anything the game classes as fogged mineable ore on the scanner's map is scannable.

[b]Q: Why is nobody using the scanner?[/b]
A: The inspect pane says why. Usually the tuned mineral is fully revealed, the scanner is roofed or unpowered, or nobody has Research work enabled.
