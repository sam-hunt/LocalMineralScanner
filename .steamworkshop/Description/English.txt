Local Mineral Scanner

[h1]About[/h1]
Never strip-mine again. A compact, pawn-operated 2x2 scanner that reveals undiscovered mineral deposits on the map you are already on. Same work per find as the long-range mineral scanner, in a smaller footprint with room for two operators.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Each find unfogs one real deposit that map generation placed; nothing is conjured
[*]Tune it to gold, silver, steel, plasteel, components, uranium or jade
[*]Two pawns can operate it at once, each at full research speed
[*]Pauses itself when no undiscovered deposits of the tuned mineral remain, and the tuning menu greys out exhausted minerals
[*]Minifiable, so it can travel to new maps; unlocked by the vanilla long-range mineral scanner research
[*]Mod settings for the roof rule, uninstalling, scan times, build cost and mass
[/list]

[b]Q: Isn't this imbalanced?[/b]
A: That's up to you. It's cheaper/smaller/lighter than Vanilla's long-distance scanner, but default find work cost is equivalent. If you want it harder, stretch the scan times or raise the costs via mod settings. If you want it easier, wind them down. Your colony, your rules.

[b]Q: Can it find ore added by other mods?[/b]
A: No. It tunes to the same list vanilla uses for precious deposits: gold, silver, steel, plasteel, components, uranium and jade.

[b]Q: Why is nobody using the scanner?[/b]
A: The inspect pane says why. Usually the tuned mineral is fully revealed, the scanner is roofed or unpowered, or nobody has Research work enabled.
